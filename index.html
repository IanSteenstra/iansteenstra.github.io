About Me

I am a research scientist and engineer focused on the safe application of AI for mental health support. I am the Co-Founder and CTO of One Small Step, leading our R&D of AI-assisted tools for certified human peer support within the Medicaid behavioral health ecosystem.

I earned my Ph.D. in Computer Science at Northeastern University, advised by Dr. Timothy Bickmore. My dissertation introduced an evaluation framework for assessing quality of care and risk in AI psychotherapy, using simulated patients to stress-test AI therapists. My work on LLM-powered counseling agents has appeared at ACM CHI and IVA, including an IVA 2024 Best Paper Award. Before my Ph.D., I was a software engineer at Wayfair, building distributed services for its customer notification platform.

  • Clinical AI safety: automated red teaming, risk ontologies, and evaluation of AI therapists.
  • Simulated users: cognitive-affective patient simulations for evaluation, counselor training, and RLAIF.
  • Conversational & embodied agents: LLM-driven virtual agents and robots for health counseling.
